# PU Realtors Website Case Study

Case study for a live real estate website co-created for PU Realtors, a plot sales and real estate business in Nagpur, India.

Live site: https://purealtors.in/

## Impact

- Helped generate ₹70 lakh+ in plot sales through digital property discovery and lead capture.
- Created a public-facing website for buyers to explore investment plots, request consultations, and book site visits.
- Turned offline real estate sales material into a structured, mobile-friendly web funnel.

## My Role

I co-created the website with my brother for our family real estate business. My work focused on:

- Website structure and page flow.
- Property landing pages.
- Buyer conversion paths.
- Trust-building content.
- Mobile-friendly presentation.
- Lead capture through call, WhatsApp, consultation, and site-visit CTAs.

## Product Scope

The site presents PU Realtors as a trusted plot advisory business and markets investment opportunities around Nagpur and Wardha Road.

Core pages and content areas:

- Home page for brand positioning and buyer education.
- Property pages for projects such as Unique City and Britain Empire.
- Investment-focused copy for Nagpur/Wardha Road growth.
- CTA flows for free consultation, phone calls, WhatsApp, and site visits.
- Trust signals including NMRDA/RERA approval messaging and bank-loan availability.
- FAQ-style information to reduce buyer friction.

## Why This Project Matters

This was not a toy landing page. It was built for a real business outcome: converting high-intent real estate buyers into leads and plot sales. The project gave me hands-on experience connecting product design, web development, content strategy, and business results.

## Tech and Tools

- Lovable

## Key Product Decisions

- Put direct lead actions near property information so interested buyers could call or message without hunting for contact details.
- Separated project pages so each property could explain its location, approvals, financing, and investment thesis clearly.
- Used approval, location, and bank-loan information as trust signals for buyers evaluating plot investments.
- Structured the site around buyer intent: discover property, understand credibility, request consultation, book site visit.
