# Architecture

The PU Realtors website is a lightweight lead-generation site built around property discovery and buyer conversion.

## Main Flows

1. Buyer lands on the home page.
2. Buyer reviews PU Realtors positioning and available projects.
3. Buyer opens a property page such as Unique City or Britain Empire.
4. Buyer reviews location, approval, financing, and investment details.
5. Buyer contacts the business through call, WhatsApp, consultation, or site-visit CTAs.

## Conversion Paths

- Call CTA
- WhatsApp CTA
- Free consultation CTA
- Site visit CTA

## Trust Signals

- Project approval messaging.
- NMRDA/RERA references.
- Bank-loan availability.
- Location and growth-story content.
- Clear business contact points.
