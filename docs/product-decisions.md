# Product Decisions

## Buyer Intent First

The website is organized around what a plot buyer needs to decide quickly:

- Where is the project?
- Is it approved?
- Is financing available?
- Why is this location valuable?
- How do I contact the seller or book a visit?

## Property Pages

Separate property pages make each listing easier to understand and share. They also make it possible to write focused content around approvals, financing, location, and investment value.

## Lead Capture

Real estate buyers often prefer direct conversation. The site therefore emphasizes high-intent CTAs such as WhatsApp, phone calls, free consultation, and site visits.

## Business Outcome

The site supported the sales funnel for PU Realtors and helped generate 70 lakh INR in plot sales.
